Academic Biography

Hi, I'm Kristy Gourab Sinha. I am a Nuclear Engineer and Materials Science Researcher. I completed my B.Sc. in Nuclear Science and Engineering from Military Institute of Science and Technology, Bangladesh in 2024 and am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Materials Science at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

My research interests include advanced materials, radiation dosimetry, alloy synthesis, corrosion analysis, materials characterization, computational materials science, nuclear instrumentation, and radiation detection and mapping. As a Research Assistant, I conduct SEM and WDXRF analyses in departmental laboratories and support undergraduate students in their research projects and thesis.
